# Homepage hero

Scope: first viewport of `/`, above the treatments strip. Mode: Experience. Build path: code-led (no image generation). Direction is brief-pinned: the user supplied a reference screenshot (giant red condensed type on black, CRT blur, scattered monospace notes, text controls along the foot) and asked for that UI with the studio's own copy, its snake logo, and every existing control kept. Answers: headline stays "More Dandyyy Less Ordinaryyy", split across the grid; colour follows the accent theme; the glass nav bar stays visible over the hero (48px there), so the hero drops its own copies of the logo, links, menu and .let's talk. Later steers: two snakes, see-through, over the words, always one on screen; neon flicker; no text over letters.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hero is a switched-on CRT: the studio's line set as wall-to-wall poster type, broken across the screen like a shout, with the logo's snake slithering through the letters. Refuses the tidy headline-plus-image hero and the floating glass chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, one rounded frame. Tanker caps in the accent (--lilac, the accent-on-dark), softly blurred and glowing, under scanlines and an aperture-grille mask. Small notes and controls in a bold typewriter face (Courier Prime), lowercase, bracketed values, `>>` and `://` marks. No glass, no pills, no icons.

STORY: Who (Dandy Studios, snake mark top centre, © line), what (designin' websites, buildin' feelings, creative branding), the voice (More Dandyyy, Less Ordinaryyy), live proof (snake, cursor dot field, camera mode), one action (.let's talk to /contact).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav bar across the top; desktop rows MORE · DANDY / YYY LESS + stepped pitch right / tags 2×2 left + ⓞRDIN / ARYYY bleeding off the bottom-right; foot at left: sound/camera, clocks, theme. Portrait: six staggered rows (tablets five, YYY LESS paired), notes in the gaps beside them; phones keep sound/camera and theme in the foot.

FORM: Brief-pinned reference rebuild; no seed key, direction pinned by the brief.

SIGNATURE: Two of the logo's snakes crawl up over the headline half a lap apart, see-through neon with a lit rim; each word flickers like a neon tube on its own rhythm; the accent dot-matrix cursor field and camera mode stay.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
