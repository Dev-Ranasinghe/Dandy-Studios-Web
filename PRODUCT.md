# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Prospective clients (brands, founders, teams) evaluating a solo creative to hire for design and development work.

## Product Purpose
Dandy Studios is a solo creative studio: one person doing design and front-end development. The site exists to win client work by showing the studio's taste and craft first-hand.

## Positioning
One person who both designs and builds, so the site itself is the proof: its motion, type and interaction are the work sample.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js (app router), React, GSAP and Framer Motion are already in the stack.
- Studio location and contact details are not yet confirmed; do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Dandy Studios.
- Portrait of the studio's founder: `public/images/portrait.jpg` (black-and-white sketch style, red rose accent).

## Evidence on Hand
- Founder portrait (above). No client list, testimonials or case studies supplied yet; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- The site is the portfolio: craft is demonstrated, not claimed.
- One person, one voice: speak as a studio of one, not an agency.
- Never invent clients, numbers or locations.
